2. Core Principle  /  核心原则 
Turn true experience into hiring evidence. Do not invent companies, education, metrics, tools, 
responsibilities, awards, or seniority. When data is missing, ask for it or mark the line as `needs 
confirmation`. 
把真实经历转化为招聘方能采信的证据。不要虚构公司、教育背景、指标、工具、职责、奖项或资历层
级。信息缺失时，要么追问，要么标注为 `needs confirmation`。 
The resume's job is to make a reviewer quickly feel: this candidate is strong, credible, and worth 
interviewing. The personal summary states the claims; experiences prove those claims. Do not make the 
reader guess the thesis. 
简历的任务，是让审阅者快速感到：这个候选人强、可信、值得面试。个人简介提出核心论点，经历部
分证明这些论点。不要让读者自己猜简历想表达什么。 
Use different evaluation logic by scenario. 
按不同求职场景使用不同评估逻辑。 
Campus recruiting: prove foundation, learning speed, project quality, internship signal, and role motivation.  
校招：证明基础能力、学习速度、项目质量、实习信号和岗位动机。 
Experienced hiring: prove role fit, business impact, scope, repeatable methods, and readiness to solve 
similar problems. 
社招：证明岗位匹配度、业务影响、负责范围、可复用方法，以及解决类似问题的准备程度。

4. Career Evidence Bank First  /  职业经历素材库优先 
Treat the user's internship, project, work, school, language, award, and portfolio records as the durable 
source of truth. A resume is only one view of that evidence. 
把用户的实习、项目、工作、学校、语言、获奖和作品记录视为长期可信的事实源。 简历只是这些证据
的一种呈现视图。 
Use the evidence bank when the user wants to record a new experience, milestone, project result, award, 
language achievement, portfolio item, or work sample. 
当用户想记录新经历、里程碑、项目成果、奖项、语言成绩、作品集条目或工作样本时，使用素材库。  
Use it to turn scattered notes into structured career evidence. 
用素材库把零散笔记整理成结构化职业证据。 
Use it to maintain a general resume while preserving richer background facts for future JD tailoring. 
用素材库维护通用简历，同时保留更丰富的背景事实，方便未来针对 JD 定制。 
Use it to generate a JD-specific resume from all stored experiences, not only from the current resume.  
用素材库从所有已存经历生成 JD 定制简历，而不是只依赖当前简历。

区分事实与润色后的简历 bullet。未确认指标必须显式标注。

5. General Resume Intake  /  通用简历信息采集 
Guide the user progressively. Ask only the next useful batch of questions instead of requiring a giant form. 
循序渐进地引导用户。每次只问下一批有用问题，不要一开始就要求填写庞大的表单。  
1. Confirm target scenario: campus, internship, experienced hire, career switch, internal transfer, or 
executive. 
1. 确认目标场景：校招、实习、社招、转行、内部转岗或高管岗位。 
2. Confirm target role family: engineering, product, design, operations, marketing, sales, data, research,

2. 确认目标岗位方向：工程、产品、设计、运营、市场、销售、数据、研究、金融、人力、咨询或其他。

7. Personal Summary  /  个人简介 
Write the summary as the resume's thesis. It should usually contain 2-3 one-line bullets. 
把个人简介写成简历的核心论点。通常包含 2-3 条一行式 bullet。 
1. Highest-value proof: the strongest recognized result, award, metric, internship, project outcome, 
industry signal, or domain credential. 
1. 最高价值证明：最强的公认成果、奖项、指标、实习、项目结果、行业信号或领域资质。  
2. Candidate trait: what kind of person the evidence proves the candidate is, with a sense of reliability and 
growth potential. 
2. 候选人特质：这些证据证明候选人是什么样的人，并体现可靠性和成长潜力。 
3. Future direction: what the candidate is moving toward and how joining the target team helps both sides 
become better. 
3. 未来方向：候选人正在走向哪里，以及加入目标团队如何让双方共同变好。 
Avoid vague self-praise. A strong summary says the candidate is good, proves it quickly, and signals they 
know how to become better. 
避免空泛自夸。强个人简介会说清候选人优秀在哪里，快速给出证据，并体现其知道如何继续成长。  
8. Project Description  /  项目描述 
Use a STAR-compatible structure. 
使用与 STAR 兼容的结构。 
1. One-line project thesis: what the project was and why it mattered. 
1. 一句话项目论点：项目是什么，为什么重要。 
2. Specific actions: what the candidate personally did. 
2. 具体行动：候选人本人做了什么。 
3. Result: what changed, preferably with metrics. 
3. 结果：发生了什么变化，最好有指标。 
4. Growth or reflection: what the candidate learned, improved, or can discuss in intervie w. 
4. 成长或复盘：候选人学到了什么、改进了什么，或能在面试中展开什么。 
Be truthful and humble. If the result is strong but not perfect, keep the resume result crisp and prepare 
interview wording that explains the context, gap vs expectation or competitors, and what would be 
improved next. Resume claims and interview stories must correspond exactly. 
保持真实和谦逊。如果结果强但并不完美，简历中保持结果表达简洁，同时准备面试话术解释背景、与
预期或竞品的差距，以及下一步会如何改进。简历主张和面试故事必须完全对应。

11. Precision Rules  /  精准表达规则 
Lead with claims, then support them with evidence. 
先提出主张，再用证据支撑。 
In each project, make the result and the trait it proves obvious before detailed evidence.  
每个项目先让结果及其证明的特质变得明显，再展开细节证据。 
One line, one sentence when possible. 
尽可能一行一句。 
Fewer stronger bullets beat many weak bullets. 
少而强的 bullet 胜过多而弱的 bullet。 
Do not display every detail; keep only evidence that supports the resume thesis.  
不要展示所有细节；只保留支撑简历核心论点的证据。 
If a skill or project does not prove strength, remove it or compress it. 
如果某项技能或项目不能证明优势，删除或压缩。 
Anything written on the resume must be explainable in an interview with matching context, action, result, 
and limitation. 
简历上写下的任何内容，都必须能在面试中用匹配的背景、行动、结果和限制来解释。

`split-bold-photo`：醒目的黑白 A4 分栏版式，灵感来自分栏参考；姓名/标题居中，强横线与竖线，左栏

The generated HTML must be printable from browser to PDF, use A4 page sizing and print CSS, and fit
into one or two A4 pages only.
生成的 HTML 必须可从浏览器打印为 PDF，使用 A4 页面尺寸和打印 CSS，并且只能控制在一到两页
A4。
If content exceeds one page, split into a second A4 page; never generate a third page. If the PDF would
exceed two pages, compress or remove lower-value content and regenerate. Do not hide overflow or
silently truncate content.
若内容超过一页，拆到第二页 A4；绝不生成第三页。如果 PDF 会超过两页，压缩或删除低价值内容后
重新生成。不要隐藏溢出内容，也不要悄悄截断。

14. JD Tailoring  /  JD 定制
When the user supplies a JD, read `references/jd-tailoring.md` and produce the following outputs.
当用户提供 JD 时，读取 `references/jd-tailoring.md` 并产出以下内容。
1. JD capability map: must-have, nice-to-have, hidden expectations, keywords.
1. JD 能力地图：必备项、加分项、隐含期待和关键词。
2. Evidence retrieval from the career evidence bank: strongest matching entries, transferable entries,
missing evidence, and risky claims.
2. 从职业经历素材库检索证据：最强匹配经历、可迁移经历、缺失证据和高风险主张。
3. Fit matrix: JD requirement, selected evidence, strength, resume action, interview risk.
3. 匹配矩阵：JD 要求、选中证据、强度、简历动作、面试风险。
4. Tailored resume changes: summary rewrite, bullet rewrites, module order changes, skills empha sis,
and deletions.
4. 定制简历修改：重写简介、改写 bullet、调整模块顺序、强化技能重点和删除内容。
5. Interview risk list: the claims most likely to be challenged.
5. 面试风险清单：最可能被追问或挑战的主张。
6. A tailored HTML resume if requested.
6. 如用户需要，生成定制 HTML 简历。
Keep a general resume and JD-specific resume separate. Never permanently overwrite the general
version unless the user asks.
通用简历和 JD 定制简历要分开保存。除非用户要求，否则绝不永久覆盖通用版本。
