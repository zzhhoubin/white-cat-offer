# Design — AI 面试助手（white_cat）

Locked design system. Studied DNA from https://www.usehallmark.com/ (structure only).

## Genre
editorial · modern-minimal hybrid

## Macrostructure
- Marketing Home: Split left-bias + numbered highlight rows + product tour
- App pages: Workbench density

## Theme
- Paper: `#fafaf8` warm near-white
- Ink: `#171412`
- Muted: `#5c574f`
- Rule: `#ddd8ce`
- Accent: `#c45c26` terracotta ≤5% (numbers, kickers, primary CTA)
- No purple gradients · no centered stacks · no icon-tile feature grids

## Typography
- Display: Fraunces + Noto Serif SC (roman only)
- Body: Noto Sans SC + Inter
- Mono: IBM Plex Mono (commands / labels)

## CTA
- Primary: solid accent terracotta
- Secondary: paper + rule border
- Ghost: paper-2

## Brand
- Wordmark: `AI` + `面试助手`
- Homepage routes to live modules only (no 自动投递)
